Decision:
  This improved WNS by 0.259 ns and TNS by 1,306.664 ns, but did not close
  200 MHz timing and required about 3.1x the baseline route time. Keep the
  default synthesis script unchanged. Do not repeat this same directive pair
  as a blind search; the remaining issue is the shared phase-to-DSP selection
  topology across layers, with routing still about 81% of the worst path.
  The next useful experiment should localize phase state by output-lane group
  and compare the mapped fanout/placement, not add another arithmetic stage.
