Decision
  Keep the bounded fanout setting in the local implementation flow because
  it improved WNS and TNS with negligible area and route-time changes. This
  is a small improvement only: the design still misses 200 MHz by about
  2.06 ns, and there is no bitstream or board acceptance. The next candidate
  must address the L5 activation/window-data route and be judged after route;
  do not repeat output-lane phase duplication or blindly sweep thresholds.
